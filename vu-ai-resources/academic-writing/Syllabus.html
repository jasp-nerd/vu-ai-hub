Welcome to the course 'Academic Writing BETA', which will start on 1 April.

Here are some important practical matters to make sure we get off to a good start:

- We will publish more information in due course, but right now we would like to ask you to sign up for your seminar groups here. Make sure you check which day you sign up for. The group numbers on Canvas match the group numbers on rooster.vu.nl. 

- The course consists of weekly lectures and seminars. Both the lectures and the seminars will take place on campus.

-Course materials for each week, as well as assignment instructions and submission links, can be found under 'Modules'.

- Attendance at the seminars is obligatory. Please sign up for a time slot that is convenient for you, and once the groups are finalised, you can contact your seminar teacher in case of issues with attendance.

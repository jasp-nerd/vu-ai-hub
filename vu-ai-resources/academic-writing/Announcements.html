  • Final assignment feedback and resit

    Posted: 6/11/2025, 10:49:27 AM

    Dear all, 

    Most of you will receive your grades for the final assignment this week, or latest early next week. Once the grades are published, please look at the teacher's evaluation of the various domains (text structure, academic style etc.) to understand the grade you have received. If you have a question about the feedback, first contact your seminar teacher instead of the course coordinator. Your seminar teacher has graded your paper, so he/she will be able to explain it best.

    As with assignment 2, you will receive one proper grade from your teacher, and default zeros from all the other teachers - please disregard the zeros and only look at the grade your teacher gave you.

    If you got a proper grade for assignment 4 that is lower than 5.5, you will have to do a resit in order to pass the course. For this course, that means rewriting the text based on the feedback. The deadline for the resit is Friday 27 June, end of the day, but for those who only get the grade next week we might add a few extra days (more information on this to follow). We will create a new submission link on Canvas. If you have a passing grade and you want to improve the grade, you need to write a completely new essay from scratch. 

    Kind regards, also on behalf of the other teachers,

    Anna

    *edited to add: your calculated, unrounded final grade for the course needs to be above 5.5 for you to pass the course; the individual components don’t all need to be above 5.5*

  • Welcome to Academic Writing (BETA); Please sign up for a seminar slot

    Welcome to the course 'Academic Writing BETA', which will start in period 5.

    Here are a few things to clarify about the structure of the course:

    The seminars for the course are all on campus, and you need to sign up for a seminar group in the people tab on Canvas. Make sure you check the day you sign up for. The seminar groups on Canvas match the seminar groups on the Rooster (though note that in some cases not the instructor). Attendance is mandatory, but if for any reason you can't attend, you should contact your seminar teacher directly and let them know you cannot make it to campus. It is then your responsibility to check the relevant slides and materials for any class that you miss.

    The lectures for the course are also on Campus. You need to attend the lectures between 10.00-10.45 on Tuesdays, at NU00C07. 

    The full course manual for this course will be made available on Canvas before the course starts (under 'Syllabus' or 'Modules'). Once the groups are finalised we will also add a discussion board with Frequently Asked Questions about the course. If you have a general question about the course, please check that discussion board first. If you have a different question and it's a general question about the course, you can add it to the discussion board. If you have a more personal/specific question, you can get in touch with your seminar teacher - again, after the groups are finalised, the contact details of the teachers will be made available. 

    We look forward to meeting you in period 5.

    Kind regards, and on behalf of all the other teachers,

    Anna

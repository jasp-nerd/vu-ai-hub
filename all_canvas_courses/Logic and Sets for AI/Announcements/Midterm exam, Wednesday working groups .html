Dear students,

the midterm exam is coming close, and for this reason next week will be dedicated for exam preparations. Specfically, in the working groups, you will discuss exercises from the example exams on the canvas page (in case you haven't seen it yet: yes, you can download exams from the previous years, which give you a good indication on how the coming exam will be like). Have a look at it, ideally before the working groups, so that we can specifically discuss the questions you have problems with. In my lecture, I will not present new content, but instead discuss questions on the lectures and exercises so far with you. If you missed a lecture, or notice there are still things you didn't understand or would like to get explained again, please come and ask me questions. Those sessions work best if you already have questions prepared.

From the attendance, it looks like this is still feasible, but it turns out that the later working groups (both Wednesday and Thursday), are often empty or almost empty - a good opportunity if you would like to practice the material in a smaller group, since the teaching assistants can there provide really targeted feedback. It is not clear to us why these sessions are so little visited - if they are conflicting with mandatory courses, please let me know. Otherwise, I think it is a missed opportunity.

Good luck preparing for the exam, enjoy your weekend and see you all on Tuesday!

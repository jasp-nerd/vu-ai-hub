Dear students,

today is the big day - I hope you are all well-prepared and feel ready for the exam! Ioana already posted some important information about the exam.

Remember that the exam takes place outside of the main campus, namely in Emergohal in Amstelveen - check connections early so that you arrive in time!

For those who are fasting for Ramadan: different to the usual regulations, you will be allowed to eat a small snack during exam hours (not a whole meal!) - you can also bring something to drink, provided that it is in a resealable bottle. Please be considerate to your fellow students, and keep noise and smells at a minimum level. 

I wish you all good luck and success!

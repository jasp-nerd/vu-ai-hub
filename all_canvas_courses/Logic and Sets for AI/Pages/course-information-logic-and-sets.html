Exercise Classes

There are two exercise classes per week: some for the Logic part on Tuesdays and Wednesdays, and some for the Sets part on Thursdays and Fridays.

For the organization of these exercise classes, there are eight Tutorial Groups, both for the Sets part and for the Logic part of the course (16 in total). You need to enroll in one Tutorial Group for Logic and one for Sets.

To enroll for these groups via Canvas, go to People, then under the Tutorial Groups for Logic tab join one of the Tutorial Groups for Logic and under the Tutorial Groups for Sets tab join one of the Tutorial Groups for Sets. The links to the groups contain the dates for the groups and the location.

Attendance of exercise classes (and lectures) is not mandatory, but highly recommended, to get properly prepared for the exams. You are supposed to take an in-depth look at the exercises beforehand, so that you can participate actively in exercise classes and can ask questions specifically about those exercises with which you experience difficulties. Usually there is not enough time for your TA to discuss all exercises during an exercise class.

Solutions to exercises in the exercise classes will not be released. (See the exams below for solutions to exam questions.)

 

Exam

There will be a midterm exam and a final exam, both covering half of the material of the Logic part, and half of the Set part of the course. The final grade will be calculated as follows: 0.4 E1 + 0.6 E2, where E1 is the first partial exam, and E2, the second.  

Practical Information on Exams

  • The first partial exam is on Monday March 3 from 15.30
  • The final exam is on Tuesday March 25 from 18.45
  • The resit exam is on Monday June 2 from 18.45.
  • Information on registering and deregistering for exams.
  • Please beware that you need to register for the resit exam!  This is only possible after the grades for the final exam have been entered into VUweb.
  • If you didn't register for an exam in time, you'll need to contact the education office.
  • General info on exams at the VU: (locations, resits, what to do in case of overlapping exams, facilities for disabled students).
  • If you registered for an exam but can't attend in the end, don't worry. Even if you miss the exam, you can still take the resit. You will not be able to take the second partial exam, though, if you do not have at least a 5 in the first partial exam.
  • Be aware that according to the examination policy of the VU, the last (so not the highest) achieved grade counts. (This is even the case if the later exam is a no show in a later academic year than when the passing grade was achieved.)

  • Should you want insight in your exam and the way it was graded you can contact the Teaching office using the following link: https://fd20.formdesk.com/vuamsterdam/aanvraag_kopie_tentamen
    The exams are usually available about 1 week after the grades have been announced.

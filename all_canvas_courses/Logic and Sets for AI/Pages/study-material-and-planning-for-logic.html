Study Material and Planning for Logic

Planning Lectures on Tuesdays

Week 1: Sections 1.1 (declarative sentences), 1.3 (propositional logic as a formal language), 1.4.1 (the meaning of logical connectives)

Week 2: Sections 1.4.3 (only definition 1.34), 1.5.1 (semantic equivalence, satisfiability and validity)

Week 3: Sections 1.5.1, 1.5.2 (conjunctive normal forms and validity)

Week 4: Midterm. No lectures. Recap working groups.

Week 5: Sections 4.4, 4.5 of Mendelson (logic circuits, binary numbers)

Week 6: Sections 6.1, 6.2 (ordered binary decision diagrams, algorithms for reduced OBDDs)

Week 7: Sections 2.1 (predicate logic), 2.2.1 (terms), 2.2.2 (formulas), 2.4.1 (models), 2.4.2 (semantic entailment)

Week 8: Exam

How to study and prepare

  • For every lecture there are slides and recorded videos.
  • Before coming to class watch the video lecture for that day and read through the slides. In class we will go through a selection of the material on the slides and combine the theory with additional examples and questions.
  • After class go over your notes and write down the question you have about the material and parts you don't understand.
  • Prepare the exercises.
  • At the tutorial/seminar/recitation you can ask questions about homework problems and additional questions you have about the material.

Study Material

  • Huth and Ryan, Chapters 1, 2, 6 : Propositional logic, predicate logic, binary decision diagrams
  • Mendelson, Sections 4.4 - 4.6 : Logic circuits

This quiz is designed to help you jog your memory on some of the "common knowledge" facts that were in the background of history. It is not necessary to know it all in detail, but knowing stuff (or realizing it) does help to put stories in perspective, and it does allow you to see some of the "obvious" connections between events. If you score 6 or better on this quiz, you're definitely up to speed; if you don't: do not despair. You probably know a lot of other facts that are just as relevant, but didn't end up in this history quiz. If you feel that other questions should be added, please send an e-mail to the professor. This is background information though, so be aware that you don't know, and look up some of the details you don't recognize while reading the book, just to obtain some more feeling for the stories.

The quiz result doesn't count to your final grade, and is intended merely as feedback for yourself.

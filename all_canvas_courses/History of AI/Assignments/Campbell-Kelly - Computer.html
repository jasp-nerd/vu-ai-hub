This quiz is designed as a study aid, to help you memorize facts and stories from the book by Campbell-Kelly. You may take it as often as you want. It doesn't have any effect on your final grade for the course. The grade you receive only reflects your knowledge of the book.

To optimize the learning effect, do reflect on the questions afterwards. For example: recall (or re-read if necessary) the stories connected to the question. Connect the wrong answers on the multiple choice questions to other stories from the book. If there is a list of names to choose from, the wrong answers are worth remembering for different reasons - other than the reason in the question, that is.

The questions are drawn ad random from the quizzes about the chapters, and three questions are drawn from a separate list of questions that focus on the book as a whole. Three questions are based on information from the lectures. The last two questions focus on general knowledge. It might be that occasionally, you come across a question that was intelligible as a question from a specific chapter, but is out of context now. If you happen to come across such a question, or any other kind of mistake or typo, please send a mail to the course coordinator, mentioning the question where you discovered the mistake.

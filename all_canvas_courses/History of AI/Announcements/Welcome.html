Welcome to this year's History of AI course.

The book used as the basis for this lecture series is Martin Campbell-Kelly's Computer: A history of the information machine, the fourth (2023) edition. This is available as an e-book at the university library. Of course you can also purchase a paper copy. We will be reading chapters 4-15. In "Modules" you may see which chapters you're expected to have prepared for each lecture.

During the lectures, the book will be touched upon, but mostly a parallel history will be brought to your attention. It is assumed, that you've read the indicated chapters before the lecture. Histories in the lectures are based on the literature, made available in the "Literature" slide of each lecture. The lecture slides will be uploaded shortly before the lecture in the appropriate place in the "Modules" section.

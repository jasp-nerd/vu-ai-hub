Posted: 3/26/2025, 8:29:13 AM

Dear students,

We'll go over the course structure in our first lecture, but here's a quick overview in advance. As you can see in the schedule below, there are four types of sessions:

  • Lecture (green) – led by Professor Beckers.

  • Lecture recap (orange) – TAs will review the previous lecture. All recap sessions cover the same material, so attending one is enough. Enroll to one of the groups in "seminar lecture recap" if you want to join.

  • Book recap & working on assignment (blue) – I will discuss the reading material and assist with the assignment. Since the deadline is right after this session, please come prepared so we can focus on final touches. Enroll to the group "seminar book + working on assignment" if you want to join. 

  • Assignment feedback (yellow) – TAs will provide feedback on the previous assignment. Like the recap sessions, these are roughly the same across different instances. Enroll to one of the groups in "seminar assignment feedback" if you want to join. 

While none of these sessions are mandatory, they are highly recommended. Alongside these, we have six assignments and the final exam (red). If your group’s average score for the four best assignments is a six or higher, you’re allowed to skip one designated question in the exam. You’ll start your exam with 2 instead of the usual 1.

Wishing you the best for your exams this week—see you on Monday!

Flora (Junior Lecturer for this course)

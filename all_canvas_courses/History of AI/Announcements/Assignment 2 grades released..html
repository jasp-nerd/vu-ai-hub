Apologises to the groups that received their grades slightly later than the other groups.

 

This week we tried to focus on answer structure as the answer for each question should be in essay format and tell a story. No bullet point answers and references aren't required.

The Alan Turing question says "To what extent" and this asks you to reflect on the positives and negatives and give reasons why you think Turing should be regarded and why he should not be regarded. The answer would conclude with a view/opinion on if Turing should be regarded as the father of AI. 
Most questions are looking for two examples, two different views (US/EU) or to reflect on an event/person.

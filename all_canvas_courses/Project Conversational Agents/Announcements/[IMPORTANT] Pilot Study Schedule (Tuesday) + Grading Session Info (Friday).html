Dear all,

This is the schedule for the pilot user study on Tuesday (January 27). Each pair is assigned a one-hour slot, during which the groups will test each other's agents. Make sure to prepare adequately and have your agent ready for testing. For details on the goal of this study, refer to this Confluence page. Pay close attention to the rooms in the schedule, since not all groups will be in their usual Tuesday room. Room HG-11A36 is reserved for TA assistance; you can go there before or after your pilot.

Moreover, during the session on Friday (January 30), your TA will grade your final conversational agent. This means your agent must be fully complete and ready for evaluation by then. The grade from this agent evaluation will count toward your final project grade. During the session, the agent will be run on a device belonging to one of your group members (not the TA’s), and the TA will test the agent directly. The schedule and assigned timeslots for this grading session will be shared in the beginning of next week via a Canvas announcement. You will have until February 1st, 23:59 to submit your final report and organize your GitHub repository. However, the agent grading itself will take place during the Friday session.

Dear Students,

Welcome to the PCA course! You will work on the project in a group of 6 students. To get things started, please ensure your group is formed and all members are enrolled on Canvas under “People” by January 6th at 13:30 (the first practical session).

Key Steps:

  1. Already Have a Group?
    Enroll all members under the same group on Canvas.
  2. Need help finding a Group?
    Join the “Assign me a group” before the deadline if you cannot find a group yourself. We will assign you a group afterwards. Failure to enroll in either the “Assign me a group” option or another student group by the deadline will be considered non-participation in the course.
  3. Incomplete Groups:
    Groups with fewer than 6 members will be filled after the deadline.

January 6th is the deadline to join or form a group. Students in “Assign me a group” will be automatically assigned to a group after this date.

We encourage you to act promptly to form a group and ensure your participation in this project. Your active involvement and individual contributions are essential for success in this course

Best regards,

Final Agent Grading

Due: 1/30/2026, 1:30:00 PM

In the final practical session of the course (Friday, Jan 30th), your TA will grade your conversational agent. This means your agent must be fully complete and ready for evaluation by then. The grade from this agent evaluation will count toward your final project grade.

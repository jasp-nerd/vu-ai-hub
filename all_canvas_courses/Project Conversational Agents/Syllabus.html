Course Description 

This project focuses on building a task-based conversational agent capable of recommending recipes from a database of nearly 1,000 options. 

The agent will use speech as its primary mode of interaction while incorporating a visual interface to display relevant information, ensuring alignment between these two modalities. Users will interact with the agent by specifying preferences and constraints, such as ingredients, cooking time, or type of dish, and the agent must understand and respond effectively to their inputs. 

A key challenge is designing an agent that can handle the variability in how users express their needs and conduct conversations while also reasoning over the broad knowledge space of recipes to provide accurate recommendations. 

The project will involve developing essential conversational capabilities, systematically testing the agent, and refining its performance. Starting with a Prolog database, a pre-trained BERT model, and a basic agent template. Teams are encouraged to add creative features to enhance the agent's functionality, culminating in a fully operational system that can assist users in finding recipes they would love to cook. In addition to the project work, there will be an onsite digital exam assessing understanding of the underlying methods and concepts.

Practical Sessions

Practical sessions are scheduled each week on:

  • Tuesdays from 15:30 to 19:15
  • Fridays from 13:30 to 17:15.

Attendance at all practical sessions is mandatory.
Missing more than two sessions will result in failure of the course. 

Attendance Rules:

  1. Check-in: arrive within the first 30 minutes of the session to check in and inform your TA of your work status.
  2. Check-out: be present within the last two hours and update the TA on your progress.
  3. A TA may require a group to stay in the room for the entire duration if the TA deems that the project's state requires attention.

You will be considered absent if you miss either the check-in or the check-out!

You must arrive within the first 30 minutes and stay for at least two hours. Of course, you are more than welcome to stay for the entire session.

If You Cannot Attend:

  • You can be excused from a maximum of two of the eight practical sessions. For these two, you do not need to provide an explanation for your absence.
  • If you miss more than two sessions, you will fail the course. 

Consequences:

Failure to comply with these rules will result in course failure, regardless of performance in other areas.

Take this seriously; no exceptions will be made.

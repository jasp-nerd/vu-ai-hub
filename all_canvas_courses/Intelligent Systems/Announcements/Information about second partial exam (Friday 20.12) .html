Information about second partial exam (Friday 20.12)

Posted: 12/14/2024, 11:42:10 AM

Please be aware that the exam will take place at the VU Sports Centrum. This is NOT at the VU Campus. More information in the attached pdf.

Please also make sure that you bring everything that is required, such as an official Identification.

While you might be allowed to register for the exam even if you did not have at least a 5.0 in the first partial exam, be aware that you are only eligible to participate in the second partial if you had at least a 5.0 in the first partial. You grade for the second exam will not be released, otherwise.

As you can imagine grading the assignments is very time intensive, and also your TAs deserve vacation after two very intense semesters. We will try to publish the results of the second partial exam before the Christmas break, but we will probably need 10 more working days in January to finish grading the practicals. This means that you cannot expect your final grade before January 16 (of course you will know more or less what grade you can expect, I'd guess once you know the exam result and the results for the majority of the practical assignments).

Good luck with the preparation, with the final assignments.

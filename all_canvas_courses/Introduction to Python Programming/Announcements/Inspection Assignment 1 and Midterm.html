Please find below the description of the procedure for the inspection of Assignment 1 and Midterm exam. For the midterm, you must have received an HTML copy of your exam. We will do the inspection of the resit exam ONLY via email.

Please follow the next steps for any clarification or questions about your grade:

  • Send an email only to IntroProgrammingAI.beta@vu.nl. Sending an email to any other email address (mine, Banno, Marin, Nimat, or any of the TAs) is not considered an official request, and therefore, will not have any validity.
  • The email's subject must be XB_0082 Inspection <XX> - ABC123, where XX is either Assignment 1 or Midterm, and ABC123 is your VUNetID. 
  • The email's body should clearly split each question or clarification into a bullet point, where you indicate your question or complaint. Please include screenshots to describe the situation, and be as precise and concise as possible. Below you'll see examples of good and bad requests.

Good requests contain clear information about what could be a potential error on our side.
✅ In task 4, three points were subtracted, but 5 points were deducted in the header.

✅ In task 1.1, points were deducted for not using a for loop, but I used a loop in lines 5-7.

✅ In task 1.4, points were deducted, but my solution is correct. I tested my code with the following examples X, Y, and Z, and it clearly demonstrates that it solves the task.

These are examples of bad requests that we have received in the past. They do not provide enough information or are not meaningful besides wanting to have a higher grade.

 ❌ In task 1.1, I should get more points.

❌ In task 2, I agree that I forgot to include a conditional, but I think I should get 3 points instead of 2.

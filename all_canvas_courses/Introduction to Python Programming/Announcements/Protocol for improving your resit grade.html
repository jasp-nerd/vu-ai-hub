Protocol for improving your resit grade

Posted: 11/27/2024, 5:38:09 PM

Dear Students,


Please find below the description of the exceptional opportunity  for you to be able to take the final exam if you obtained less than 50 in the midterm. This applies exclusively to students that participated in the midterm exam. If you did not attend the midterm, unfortunately, this does not apply to you.

The process is pretty simple. There are three tasks that you must fulfil within the specific deadlines. These tasks are meant to be developed at home. If you fail to fulfil  any of the requirements, you will not be allowed to take the final exam, and then you have to wait until the course’s resit.

Given the nature of the tasks, there’s one additional condition. These tasks must be developed individually, following the academic integrity principles of the VU. Therefore, if you copy, share, cooperate, collaborate, etc., or let someone copy, see, get inspired from your solutions, this is considered plagiarism. If there’s a small sign of or attempt of copy, this opportunity will have no validity, and everyone who obtained less than 50 will have to wait until the resit, as mentioned in the course’s study guide. So just make sure that you do not copy and do not let anyone copy from you 😉.

You can always hand in any of the tasks before the deadline, but not after. There are **no** exceptions due to any type of circumstance. Again, this is an exceptional opportunity, so if you have any programmed trips, events, weddings, birthdays, etc. there's no flexibility with the deadlines.

 

## Task 1: Correction

**Deadline: ** November 29th by 15:00.
**Drop-off Location: ** TBD

You will have to make a correction in pen and paper of your exam. The one you received as HTML. To do so, you have to print the exam we sent you (including your answers) in A4 format and correct/fix the mistakes you made during the exam. If you don’t have a printer at home, you can print at the VU.
* If you did not do a task, add a solution 
* If your solutions are too chaotic, you don’t have space, then you can write a correct solution next to it.

## Task 2: Practice

**Deadline: ** November 29th by 15:00.
**Drop-off Location: ** TBD

You will have to solve a programming exercise that we will publish on November 25. This exercise must be printed in A4 format and solved in pen and paper. 
You have to write your name and VUNetId.

## Task 3: Peer Review

**Deadline: ** December 5th by 17:00.
**Drop-off Location: ** TBD

On December 2nd from 12:00-13:00, you will pick up another classmate’s exercise (Task 2), which you will have to grade and give feedback about the correctness of their solution. Please give constructive feedback, highlighting the positive and the negative aspects of the solution. You have to write your name and VUNetId as reviewer.

#  Result:

As a result of successfully completing these three tasks and that everyone behaves ethically according to the VU’s Academic Integrity, you will:

  • Obtain a 5.0 in the midterm exam.
  • Be allowed to take the final exam.

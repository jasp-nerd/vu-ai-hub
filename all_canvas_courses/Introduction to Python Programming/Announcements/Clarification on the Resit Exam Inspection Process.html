Dear students,
I’d like to take a moment to clarify the details regarding the upcoming inspection to ensure everything is clear for you:
  • The inspection takes place in person at the university (room: NU-4B47) on Monday 31st of March from 14:00-16:30.
  • You must follow the procedure explained in the previous email.
  • We won’t be answering questions via email or Discord. If you have any concerns, please follow the procedure, and we’ll be happy to assist you.
  • Keep in mind that the inspection is not a negotiation. Mistakes can happen, and if there’s an error in the grading, we’ll gladly review and correct it. However, if your request is in the line “I need X points to pass the course. Can you check again? Unfortunately, the answer is no. You should check if there is or not an error in the grading. If there is, we’re more than happy to check your concrete request.
  •  If you obtained zero in a task could be for two main reasons:
    •  You did not hand in the right notebook with the answer, or
    • You did not answer the question within the marks:

  • When checking your exams, please take into account that you start with 0 points, not 100. Concretely for question 4, you have to propose a solution. Therefore, each of you can make unique errors, that’s why in this, the sum of the errors could be higher than the points of the question if you commit many different errors.

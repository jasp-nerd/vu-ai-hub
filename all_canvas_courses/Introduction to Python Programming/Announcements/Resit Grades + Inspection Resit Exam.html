Inspection Resit Exam

The inspection of the resit exam will be only in person. The inspection will take place at the university (room: NU-4B47) on Monday 31st of March from 14:00-16:30. Please find below the description of the procedure for the inspection. Please keep in mind that inspection will take place in person at the university; after this period, questions will not be answered.

Please follow the next steps to request any clarification/questions/correction about your grade:

  • Print your exam in an A4 format.
  • You must highlight on the exam the issue/comment/concern you want us to revisit.
  • In an additional A4 paper, you must write down:
    1. The question/comment/concern/etc.  you have on a task in the exam (e.g., Task 2 was not graded. Can you please clarify this?). 
    2. Motivation on why this is an issue/error/doubt/etc. (e.g., I obtained 0 in Task 2 but I have a partial correct implementation but I obtained 0; so it seems my task was not graded.).
    3. Your expected outcome of the request (e.g., I expect this to increase my grade by X points because this question was not graded).

Please keep in mind that you must follow the previous steps; If you don't follow them, your request will not be taken into account.

Dear students,

We already processed all the submissions for the extra opportunity for the midterm exam. As explained in the description of the task, there were four tasks:
  1. Correction of the midterm.
  2. Solution to the problem.
  3. Tests ( week 9)
  4. Reflection.
As explained in the task, "If you fail to fulfill any of the requirements, you will not be allowed to take the final exam, and then you have to wait until the course’s resit." 
In the next spreadsheet, you can see six columns: vunetid, correction (this is the correction of the midterm), solution (this is the solution of the extra task), tests (unit tests for the extra task), reflection, and total. ✅ In the total column, you must have 4 to be eligible for the peer-review and to take the final exam (if you complete the peer review).
🔗 Spreadsheet
‼️ If you have less than 4, that means that you did not fulfill the requirements, and therefore, you do not have to do the peer review (you do not have to come on Monday) and are no longer eligible to take the final exam (as written in the study guide). 
The reasons for not getting the four points are:
  • You missed handing in one or more tasks (you can check the table to see which part(s) you missed).
  • You handed in a wrong solution for a task. For example, the tests are not unit tests as taught in the course (Week 9). Instead of writing unit tests (valid Python code), you wrote paragraphs in natural language explaining your solution, or only input and output values.

Syllabus — Introduction to Python Programming

👋🏽 Welcome to Introduction to Python Programming (2024-2025)

During this course, you will learn how to write programs for solving problems in Python using different data types (e.g., int, boolean, float, string, and custom types), data structures (e.g., lists, sets, dictionaries, and tuples), expressions, assignment statements, if-statements, iterations (while- and for-statements), and comprehensions. Also, you will learn how to use built-in functions, as well as how to define functions, perform I/O tasks, and Object-Oriented Programming (OOP) concepts.

The course consists of one hour and 45 minutes of lectures and practical session per week. Lectures take place on Wednesdays at 11:00 in Room MF-FG1 (see map), and practical sessions vary depending on your group (see the schedule below).

💯 Examination & Grading

The assessment of the student's progress in this course takes place via exercises, quizzes, assignments, midterm exam, and final exam.

The grading of the course is as follows:

  • Practical assignments or in-class quizzes constitute 10% of the final grade.
  • Mid-term exam constitutes 25% of the final grade. Students must obtain at least a 5.0 on the midterm exam to be able to take the final exam.
  • Final exam constitutes 65% of the final grade.
    • A minimum grade of 5 in the Final exam is required to pass the course.
  • [optional] All exercises-with a score of at least 80% 85%-count for 5% of the course's final grade only if you obtain a grade higher than 5 in the mid-term and final exam.
  • The passing grade of the course is 5.5 (for more information please check Teaching and examination regulation 2023-2024)

 

⏳Assignments and Exercises

Exercises and Assignments are published on Wednesdays at 14:00. The deadline for the exercises is the next Tuesday by 23:59, and for the assignments is Wednesdays at 23:59. This is a hard deadline, therefore, no submissions are accepted after this date. For example, if an assignment is released on September 11th, this has to be handed in by September 20th at 23:59.

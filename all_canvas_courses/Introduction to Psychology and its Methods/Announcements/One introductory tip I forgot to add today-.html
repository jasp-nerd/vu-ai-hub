Hi all,

While I was chatting with some of you after class, I realized that I should give you this one tip on studying for this course.

The best way of understanding the topics we discuss is learning them example based. However, I would like to share a word of caution when it comes to using chatgpt (or other gen AI) as a study aid. 

Most of us are using chatgpt, for questions ranging from "What is a good name for a cat that chirps?", "How can I protect my cables form young kittens?" or "Can the fleas on my kittens jump on me?". It can also be helpful as a study assistant, simplifying concepts and coming up with examples.

PLEASE NOTE THAT my experience with it when it comes to asking it for definitions of terminology or examples of concepts is a bit problematic. It often comes up with incorrect examples and make logical mistakes. When you use it, try to first come up with your own answers based on the study materials, and then compare it to chatgpt responses. Try to have a third source to compare.

There is always a chance you are simply more effective than me in using it, however, do be careful!

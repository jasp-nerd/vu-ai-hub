Dear students,

We have published the grades of all assignments and the exam in Canvas and uploaded the final grades to the VU Dashboard.

Explanation of grades

Grades are calculated by taking 40% of the average grade for the homework, 20% of the grade of the final assignment, and 40% of grade for the exam. If you did not participate in the exam, you will see a 'NS' (no show). If you have a grade lower than 5,5 for either the average of the homework assignments, the final assignments, or the exam, you will see a 'NVD' (insufficient).

Redoing one assignments

There is an opportunity to redo at most one assignment if this would make it possible for you to pass the course. If you want to do so, please enroll in the group 1 ASSIGNMENT RESUBMISSION registration before Tuesday May 6 end of day. We will check whether you are eligible (based on the possibility to pass the course and your participation in at least 5 our of the 7 working groups). If you are eligible, you can resubmit via the assignment "Resit Assignment" (will be visible after May 6). The deadline for resubmission is Monday May 12 end of day.

Review of exam

We will organize a review session for the exam in a digital examination room. We will announce the location and the time slot in the week of May 5 at latest.

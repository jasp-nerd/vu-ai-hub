2. Aplysia ink releasing model

In the second half of this assignment, we are going to design another model related to the Aplysia behavior, namely its defense mechanism.

Consider the following description of this part of the process. Aplysia’s defensive mechanism consists of the release of a red ink (see the image on the first page of Chapter 4). When the Aplysia senses it is in danger, several mechanisms act at the same time to result in a very fast response. The concepts used in the model of the defensive mechanism consist of an external event, i.e., "danger"; neuronal processes, which are named "sensory process", "motor processes", "internal mechanisms 1" and "internal mechanisms 2"; and an action, i.e. "ink release". Thus, danger in the environment is sensed by a group of sensor neurons. Then, two mechanisms start acting at the same time. The result is the activation of a group of motor neurons and the release of ink.

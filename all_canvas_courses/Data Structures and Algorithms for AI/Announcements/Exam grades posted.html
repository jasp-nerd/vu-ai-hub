I decided to adjust the final score, to reflect this, and improve your grades. Your final grades are based on the following formula:

Total points earned in the exam (out of 70) + Highest problem scored (scaled out of 11.5)

Any points over 70 were truncated, and will not be carried over to future exams.

 

As an example if you received: 

Problem 1 (out of 18 points): 14 points

Problem 2 (out of 10 points): 8 points     

Problem 3 (out of 16 points): 5.5 points

Problem 4 (out of 12 points): 9 points

Problem 5 (out of 14 points): 13 points  (highest problem score)

-----------------------------------------------

Total score =  5 + 8 + 5.5 + 9 + 13  = 49.5

Final score = 49.5 + 13 * ( * 11.5) = 60.2 out of 70 possible points

Dear students,

Assignment 1 is up (due 10 November).

During the first practical you will form a pair (with some one from the same slot and room). You need self-enroll in a group on the People page. If for what ever reason you cannot not find a partner, sign up in a group of 1. If you are not in a group before Thursday 23.59h we assume you are not taking the course and will be removed from Canvas.

Refined attendance rules
Attendance is mandatory. You have 2 slip days. If you are >15 min late, your attendance is set to 50%. You can only leave early when there is no more work to do (e.g. you submitted all the available assignments). In all other cases, your attendance is set to 50% if you leave early. You need to have at least 5/7 attendance (visible via grade book) to pass the course.

Grade transfers
If you did apply for a grade transfer the above steps do not apply for you. Do not enroll in a group. You will not be removed from Canvas. Instead we will pair you up, unless indicated that you prefer to do the assignments alone or we cannot find a suitable match for you. Again you are excused from the mandatory attendance, but are more than welcome to (and we highly recommend that) to join the practicals.

Good luck with the assignments!

The resit of the quizzes is on December 19th at 11:00 AM. If the average score of the two quizzes is lower than 55% (count the percentages of the two quizzes and divide by two) or if you missed one of the two quizzes, you can do the resit (see also the study guide). The resit covers the entire course material. You must have at least 55% of the questions correct to pass the resit. If you do not pass the resit, you will have to retake the course next year.

Register for the resit group (see People in the menu) if you want to take the resit. Do this before December 17th. We will check whether you are eligible.

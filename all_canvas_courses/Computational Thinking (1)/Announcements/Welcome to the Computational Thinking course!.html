Hello everyone,

Welcome to the Canvas page of "Computational Thinking". In this course, you will be introduced to different problem-solving strategies and algorithms.

The "Computational Thinking" course revolves around self-study with video lectures in combination with classroom meetings (once per week) for deepening understanding and practical sessions (once per week). 

Therefore, we ask you to watch the videos before coming to these classes and starting with the practicum assignment. There is no point in attending class meetings and practicum sessions if you have not prepared yourself by watching the videos and reading the topics in the syllabus. All documents for this course (web lectures, slides, assignments, etc.) are listed in the modules section.

Before starting the course, we urge you to read the "OK and not OK" document, the study guide, and watch the "Think before you send" video. That way you know what we expect from you.

Practical assignments are released one week before the deadline. This way you have enough time to work on your assignments. We ask you to enroll in a group so that you can participate in the practicum sessions and the project assignment. The practicum group is also your project group. During the practicum sessions, a teaching assistant (TA) will be present to help you with the questions about the practicum assignments. The practicum sessions are scheduled for Thursdays.

The practicum sessions are not compulsory to attend. But it is mandatory to hand in your assignment every week. Consult the study guide about this.

Explore this site further by clicking on the various buttons to see what information is available for you. This way you will quickly find the information you need for this course.

Good luck!

Hi everyone,

On November 21, you will have your first quiz. The quiz covers all the material you've had so far (weeks 1-3). The quiz will be in Canvas and is multiple-choice.

You can prepare for the quiz by:

- studying chapters 1 - 6 in the syllabus.
- watching the videos from weeks 1 - 3.
- studying the lecture slides.
- studying the assignments from weeks 1 - 3 (you must be able to solve the problems by yourself).

In addition to theory questions, you will be presented with some simple Python code to tell, for example, whether the code is right or wrong or what the output of the code is. So, you must be able to read and follow the Python code. You must also be able to apply the theory to problems.

You can only take the quiz on campus. 

For the quiz, you are not allowed to use websites or devices such as calculators, mobile phones, or similar. You may use a pen and paper for calculations. We've created a practice quiz (see quizzes button) for you to give you an idea of what the quiz will look like and what kind of questions you can expect.

Good luck with the preparations.

We have observed that some group members are not actively contributing or responding to messages regarding the group project. This is a crucial reminder that active participation is mandatory for successful completion of the course project.

If you have a teammate who is not contributing or communicating, please notify your assigned TA via email as soon as possible. Please ensure the following when reporting:

  1. Do not include their name in the final project report.
    Their lack of participation will be noted, and they will receive a score of 0 for the project.

  2. Be detailed and honest in your email.
    Provide a clear explanation of their lack of involvement, including specific instances if possible. This helps us address the issue appropriately.

Non-participating students will face serious consequences, including a potential failure of the course due to their failure to meet the project requirements.

This policy ensures fairness and accountability among all students. We trust that you will maintain professionalism and integrity in reporting any concerns.

Thank you for your cooperation. If you have any questions, please reach out to your TA or course coordinators.

Practical Information

Some important practical information:
  • The research project will be presented in detail during the first lecture. 
  • The project will be performed in groups of 5 students.  You will get support and guidance from an academic member of staff, sometimes called your tutor
  • There will be a meeting between students and tutor every week on Wednesday, Thursday or Friday. See the calendar of this course. Every phase of your project has a preparation session (you come up with ideas, questions, etc.), and a presentation session (every group present their results).
  • Every assignment has to be submitted (see deadlines in the assignments), and tutors will provide them with feedback (by mail or in the tutorial sessions).
  • Of course you should meet more often without the tutor.
  • The project results have to be finished and presented. 

For the schedule of the lectures and workgroup meetings see: https://rooster.vu.nl

There will be one on-campus meeting of your tutor-group with your tutor at the VU. Details will be communicated by your tutor.

Intro AI in a nutshell

There is a lot of information available, and more information (like slides) will be added during the course. Here is what you need to do to pass:

Scheduled activities (check  the calendar and https://rooster.vu.nl/ for authorative information):

  1. Lectures. There will be three methodological lectures and several guest lectures to introduce some of the main ideas and concepts of AI.
  2. Mentor Groups: There will be one 2 hour session with an acedemic mentor in groups of ~20 students, which are devoted to your academic development, practical problems related to the study as well as research contributing to the final research projects (see next bulletpoint).
  3. Research Groups: You will work in groups of 5 students from your Mentor groups on a research project.

Group deliveries (group assignments to be submitted via Canvas, check the Assignments tab and the calendar)

  • Draft (weekly): short documents to prepare the discussion on parts of your project in the mentor group
  • Final (weekly): updated versions of the draft after feedback
  • Final Project: you need to submit a scientific report, video, poster and slides of a presentation

Individual deliveries: (individual assignments to be submitted via Canvas, check the Assignments tab and the calendar)

  • Reflection on the Guest Lectures
  • Peer and self-review of contribution (more information later).

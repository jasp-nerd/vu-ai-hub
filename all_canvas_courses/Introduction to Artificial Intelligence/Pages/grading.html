Grading

For each of the report, poster and video you will get a grade for the entire group.

There is a small individual component.

The final grade will be calculated based on the groups' grade for:

Group Project 40%
Video 5 %
Poster 5 %
Exam 30 %
Computer Basics' Assignment 10 %
Diversity Class' Assignment 10%
Total 100%

We will apply the following criteria for marking:

  • Report (each criteria count for 1/5 of the mark):
    • the general structure and style of the report
    • the creativeness of the problem, and the quality of the problem description
    • the quality of the literature overview (knowledge acquisition with expert is a bonus)
    • the description of the solution
    • the quality of the proposed evaluation method
  • The mark for the presentation will be determined by the structure and content of the presentation, and the clarity (and effectiveness) of the poster.
  • The mark for the video will be determined by its creativeness and communicational strength (while being accurate). The technical quality of the video does not play a role.

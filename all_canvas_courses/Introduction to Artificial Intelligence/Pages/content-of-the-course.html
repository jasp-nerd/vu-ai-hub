Content of the Course

In a number of lectures the basic ideas and techniques behind Artificial Intelligence will be presented. 

In small groups of students under supervision of a staff member (tutor) you will propose a practical problem, analyse this problem through the study of scientific literature and contact with domain experts, devise a potential solutions for it, and identify a methodology for validating your solution in an experimental way.

 Introduction to Artificial Intelligence is, as the name suggests, an introduction. Once you've followed the course, we hope you will have a better insight into what AI is all about, and how the Bachelor AI at the VU is organised.

There are four aspects to this introduction.

  1. in order to get a first glimps at AI methods, we will give a number of lectures regarding some more methodological issues: how to find literature, how to elicit knowledge from experts,and how to validate a scientific hypothesis in experiments.
  2. a first glance into some of the algorithmic solutions AI can offer.
  3. general overviews over some of the main AI sub-disciplines, and those in particular on which research is done at the VU, and finally
  4. in order to get some feeling for the practical aspects of AI, you will work in groups on a research project.

What do you have to do (see also "Assignments"):

  • There will be guest lectures: listen and ask questions.
  • There will be methodological lectures. Here you will get inspiration for possible problems, and learn the skills you need for finishing the research project succesfully.
  • There is a research project you will work on throughout the course in groups of 5 with a tutor.
    • Regular home-work
    • The final project will be a report (based on the home-work from weeks 2-6), a presentation with poster and a video.

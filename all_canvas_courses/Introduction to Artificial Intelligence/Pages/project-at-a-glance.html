Project at a glance

Goal: To make you identify a (societally relevant)  problem for which Artificial Intelligence can offer (partial) solutions, analyse this problem given existing literature and knowledge acquisition, design and present a solution and devise a validation strategy to evaluate the quality of your solution.   

Final deliverable: For the final assignment you will have to deliver in groups of 5 students the following:

  1. A report (~10 pages) covering the following aspects:
    - What is the problem? (1 page)
    - What has been done? A literature overview (3-5 pages)
    - How can we solve the problem? A description of a potential solution (2 pages)
    - Is the solution valid? A description of an evaluation strategy, experimental setup. (2 pages)
  2. A "commercial" poster describing the problem
  3. A video (~90 second) describing the problem, solution, the scientific questions and the experimental setup.  
At the end of the course, each project (which is done in groups of 5 students) must be ready to present and submit slides. There will be a competition for the Best Intro AI project, where a chosen number of projects is allowed to present their projects.
Objective: to give you an overview over the core aspects of life as a Artificial Intelligence professional: understanding a problem in a specific domain, designing intelligent technology to solve the problem, and scientifically assessing the quality of this solution. 
Disclaimer: given the scope of this class all aspect can only be very superficially touched. No system will be built, no experiments performed. This is for later in the study, but we hope the project gives you an overview over what Artificial Intelligence is going to be.

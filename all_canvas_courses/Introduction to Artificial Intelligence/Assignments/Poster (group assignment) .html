Poster (group assignment)

Due: 10/11/2024, 11:59:59 PM

Part of your future work will be to promote yourself and your work. Usually you will be given very little time to convince a client or other researchers that you are working on an interesting and important problem, and it is crucial to be able to reduce your message to some strong but concise points, and to present it in an appealing way. 

We want you to produce a poster that attracts a wide audience to the problem you want to solve. Make sure you identify both the general domain and the specific problem you want to address. Use strong visual means.  

You can make your poster in A3-format (using Powerpoint) and submit it to canvas (pdf).
Depending on the corona situation, we will held the poster session :
  • physically, in which case you can print the poster on the printers in the NU building.  
  • remotely, in which case you will present the poster on Zoom.

NB This is a group assignment, only one has to submit for the group.

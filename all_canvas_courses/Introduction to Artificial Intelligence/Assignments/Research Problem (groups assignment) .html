Research Problem (groups assignment)

Due: 9/13/2024, 11:59:59 PM

Write in 1 A4 what your choice is for the scope of your project (the "research problem") and what the problem is that you want to solve within that domain (the "task"). 

Examples:

  • Support for deafening by converting sound to image,
  • Elderly support when using email,
  • Detecting depressive people on Facebook
  • ...

Tip: Think in your own environment, whether there are people who you could help with an Artificial Intelligence solution.

Take into account: domain, problem, input/output description (of the application), the knowledge that is needed.

 

NB This is a group assignment, so just one of you will have to submit.

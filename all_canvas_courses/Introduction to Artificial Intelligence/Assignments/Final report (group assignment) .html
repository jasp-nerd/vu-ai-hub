Final report (group assignment)

Due: 10/31/2024, 11:59:59 PM

The report should consist of about 12 pages (in English).
It should be written as a scientific document. We suggest you use the following (indicative) structure: 
  • Abstract (1/2 page): summarise each of the points below in 1 sentence.
  • Introduction (1 page) what is this report about, what are the research questions, and what is the contribution of the report? 
  • Research Problem (2 pages): Describe and motivate the problem you want to address. This includes a description of the domain and the specific problem at hand. Think of an audience that is not familiar with the domain, so provide sufficient background to make it abundantly clear what your target is.  
  • Literature Review (3 pages): What has been done Provide an overview over the related literature, and a description of an approach to collect sufficient information from experts required to solve it.
  • Approach (3 pages):  how will you solve the problem? Focus on the algorithmic and interactive aspects of a piece of technology that might solve the problem. This technology does not need to exist, nor does it necessarily be realistic to expect that it can exist within the near future. Be bold, be creative, but make sure that the solution is somehow viable. 
  • Experimental Evaluation (2 pages): explain the evaluation you plan for your solution. Of course this is a proposal not a concrete evaluation, i.e. describe how will set up necessary experiments, how many people would you invite, which groups, which technologies and metrics you will use etc. 
  • Conclusion (1page): what did you do, and what did we learn? Are your research questions answered?

Of courses, these are exactly the topics you worked on during the course, so you should reuse as much of the material as you deem relevant.

 

  • It is highly desired that you use Springer style formatting in the style of the Springer Publications format for Lecture Notes in Computer Science (LNCS). For details on the LNCS style, see Springer’s Author Instructions (Links to an external site.).
  • It is highly recommended  that you use Latex for this. With Overleaf (Links to an external site.)  you have a very nice platform for writing Latex collaboratively. It is worth investing time in this. The easiest is probably to start from this Overleaf LCNS template.

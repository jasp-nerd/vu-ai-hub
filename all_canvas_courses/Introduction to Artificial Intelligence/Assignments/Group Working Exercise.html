Group Working Exercise

Due: 9/13/2024, 8:00:00 PM

This exercise is meant to be done during your working session of week 2.

In each project group:

  • each member writes at least 3 expectations (ex. I want cum laude, I expect this assignment to be difficult for me because I am not interested in this subject...)
  • share the expectation with your colleagues
  • create at least 5 ground rules for interaction that you intent to use in your project group
  • submit on canvas as a group assignment before the end of the session

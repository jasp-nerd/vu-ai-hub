Hello everyone,

I hope your year is off to an amazing start, and that you're ready for some fantastic lectures and tutor groups ahead.

We'd like to remind you about our code of conduct and emphasize the importance of respecting one another. The University takes this very seriously, and we strive to create an environment where everyone feels welcome and safe.

Please keep these important principles in mind:

1. **Honesty**: Uphold intellectual and personal honesty in all aspects of learning, teaching, research, and service.
2. **Trust**: Foster mutual trust by encouraging and supporting the free exchange of ideas.
3. **Fairness**: Promote fairness in interactions among students, faculty, and administrators.
4. **Respect**: Honor, value, and consider diverse opinions and ideas.
5. **Responsibility**: Take action when you encounter any form of wrongdoing.
6. **Courage**: Stand up for your values, even when facing pressure and adversity.

If any issues arise, please don’t hesitate to reach out for support:

- For class-related concerns, speak directly with your teacher(s).
- For resources and support, connect with your study advisors.
- For student well-being and concerns, reach out to the department diversity office.
  - Office hours: Every 3rd Monday of the month, 12-1 pm (lunch hours)
  - Email: diversity.cs.beta@vu.nl

You can also find more information about academic integrity here: [Academic Integrity]

Let's work together to make this class a safe and supportive space for everyone!

Wishing you all the best,

Francesca

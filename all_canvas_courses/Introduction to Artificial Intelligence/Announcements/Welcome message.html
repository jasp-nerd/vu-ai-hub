Welcome to the BSc Artificial Intelligence, and the course Introduction to AI.

The course is meant as a general introduction to the field of Artificial Intelligence, and more specifically the Bachelor Programme. 

The two lectures per week are given to the full group of more than 250+ new students on campus.

Furthermore, you registered with one Tutorgroup (also called Section/Sectie or Group/Groep) with 24 other students. Please carefully check on www.rooster.vu.nl in which group you are, and when you should come. You will also be randomly assigned to a ProjectTeam of 5 students, in which you will work on the course project during your tutor group sessions.

We look forward to seeing you all on Monday, Sept. 2nd and more and more during the coming months.

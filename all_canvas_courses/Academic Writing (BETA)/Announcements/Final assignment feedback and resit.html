Dear all, 

Most of you will receive your grades for the final assignment this week, or latest early next week. Once the grades are published, please look at the teacher's evaluation of the various domains (text structure, academic style etc.) to understand the grade you have received. If you have a question about the feedback, first contact your seminar teacher instead of the course coordinator. Your seminar teacher has graded your paper, so he/she will be able to explain it best.

As with assignment 2, you will receive one proper grade from your teacher, and default zeros from all the other teachers - please disregard the zeros and only look at the grade your teacher gave you.

If you got a proper grade for assignment 4 that is lower than 5.5, you will have to do a resit in order to pass the course. For this course, that means rewriting the text based on the feedback. The deadline for the resit is Friday 27 June, end of the day, but for those who only get the grade next week we might add a few extra days (more information on this to follow). We will create a new submission link on Canvas. If you have a passing grade and you want to improve the grade, you need to write a completely new essay from scratch. 

Kind regards, also on behalf of the other teachers,

Anna

*edited to add: your calculated, unrounded final grade for the course needs to be above 5.5 for you to pass the course; the individual components don’t all need to be above 5.5*

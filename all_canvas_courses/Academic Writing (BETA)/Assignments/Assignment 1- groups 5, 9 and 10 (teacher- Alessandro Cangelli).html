Write a 3-sentence ‘elevator pitch’ to introduce what you want to write your final essay on. You can choose your own articles or draw inspiration from the article database on Canvas. Choose according to your interest, because this is the topic you will be working with for the rest of the course. The aim of this assignment is to start the process of thinking about the articles as comparative texts, and to check if academic formality and accuracy work efficiently in your writing.  Also make sure that you mention the academic papers and indicate some connections between them.

  1. Include three sentences following this format:
  2. Sentence one should give the reader a general idea of what you’re writing about:

    In recent years, many studies have been published on the use and influence of social media.

    In recent years, the number of Facebook users has increased drastically.

     

  3. Sentence two should narrow the general topic down and/or introduce a problem:
    One of the aspects that has been studied is the effect of social media on psychological well-being of teenagers.

    However, there are rising concerns about the privacy of Facebook users.


  4. Sentence three should explain what the aim is of your text, so how you see the articles in dialogue with each other:

    In fact, there is strong evidence that social media have a negative influence on teenagers’ self-esteem.

    How can the data of Facebook users be protected?

     

Please don't submit more than 3 sentences. If you get a fail, you will need to hand in a new version within a couple of days.

Instructions for students for Assignment 3 (deadline 9 May)

Important:
Check who your seminar instructor is. Incorrect submissions will not be graded.
Include your name in the document name and in the document itself.

See the Week 6 module for information about how to complete this assignment. Write 3 sentences following the framework outlined in the materials. It would also be helpful to include your thesis statement in your assignment, but no need to submit your entire essay.

  1. Include three sentences following this format:
  • Sentence one should remind the reader of or recap the thesis statement 
  • Sentence two should synthesize your findings to answer the main question in your thesis statement 
  • Sentence three should provide an outlook, broadening up the topic again by either discussing the implications of the current findings and/or by providing specific suggestions for further research
  1. If you get a fail, you will need to hand in a new version within a couple of days.

Some general tips:

1) Study well-edited published examples (e.g., https://www.nature.com)

2) Use a collocations dictionary, such as OZDIC.com

3) Use the Chicago Manual of Style (but only for questions concerning grammar and punctuation) and check every comma

4) Use the Academic Phrasebank: https://www.phrasebank.manchester.ac.uk/

5) Read the relevant chapters on our ELS Online website and consult our slides, videos, and class materials before you submit

FAQs about the course

Which components of the course will be on campus?

The weekly lectures and seminar groups will take place on campus, according to the schedule on rooster.vu.nl.

Will there be a hybrid option for seminars?

No, we have decided to follow the guidelines of the university and move our seminars back to fully on campus, since we believe fully in-person classes offer the best overall learning experience. If during the course there’s a week that you’re ill or you have to quarantine, you should contact your seminar teacher directly and let them know you cannot make it to campus. It is then your responsibility to check the relevant slides and materials for any class that you miss.

 

How do I get in touch with my seminar teacher?

You can find their email addresses here. Alternatively, you can ask questions in person during or after your seminars.

 

Can I change seminar groups?

No.

 

What if I miss too many seminar sessions? 

Please check the course manual for details. If there is a week where you are ill and you cannot come to campus, it is your responsibility to inform your seminar teacher and to review the materials for any classes that you missed. Additionally, if there are personal circumstances that make it difficult for you to attend enough sessions, make sure you get in touch with your seminar teacher. Please note that exceptions to the normal requirements for attendance and participation can only be made if you also get in touch with a study advisor.  

 

I have to retake the course, can I use my grades and/or attendance from last year?

No, unfortunately that's not possible. If you have to retake the course, the same rules and requirements apply as for students who take the course for the first time. This means that you need to submit all three required assignments. Please also note that you can't use texts that you submitted last year (this would be self-plagiarism). As for attendance, you can ask your seminar teacher if it's ok for you to miss more sessions - but note that keeping up with the course content is your own responsibility. 

 

I am registered but will not participate, can you remove me from the course?

As teachers we can't influence registration (either enrolling or excluding students from a course). It would be helpful to just send a quick message to Anna Martonfi (a.martonfi@vu.nl), so we know that you're not participating.

 

I have a lot of writing experience, can I get an exemption?

You can only get an official exemption for the course if this has been granted by the examination board. You will have to submit an official request and please note that it may take a while for them to process this, so this should really be done well before a course starts. Exemption for this particular course is usually only granted if you have followed a similar English and academic writing course at a different university. If you do not have an official exemption all regular requirements for attendance and completing the assignments apply.

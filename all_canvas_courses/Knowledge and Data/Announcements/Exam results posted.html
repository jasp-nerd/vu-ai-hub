The exam results are calculated as follows: The exam has a maximum total of 34 points. The chance score is 8.50. This means that if you have 22.53 (23) or more points, you pass the exam with a 6.0 or higher, rounded to 0.5. Scores below that result in a 5 or lower, rounded to 0.5.

The exam is one of the elements required to be passed to pass the course.

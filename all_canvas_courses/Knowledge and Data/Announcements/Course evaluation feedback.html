Course evaluation feedback

Posted: 11/4/2025, 9:20:30 AM

Thanks all of you who filled out the evaluation form! Even though there were only 18 completed forms, we still see some patterns that we will take on board to improve next year's edition!

What you liked

  • Quizzes were widely appreciated for helping students check understanding and prepare for the exam.
  • Practical assignments were seen as engaging and useful for learning to apply concepts.
  • Students found the lectures clear and well-structured.
  • TA support in working groups was generally valued when it helped clarify material.
  • Many felt the course was well-organized and balanced in its current form (except timing of the project).

 

What Needs Improvement / Change

  • The project overlapped too much with exams and caused workload stress. -> we will look at the timing of the project next year
  • Several students found the Semantic Web topic outdated and suggested focusing on more modern or practical areas (e.g., knowledge graphs, LLMs, or RAG systems) -> We will investigate further inclusion of LLMs for ontology engineering or RAG systems
  • Working Groups received mixed feedback: some found them helpful, others said they were repetitive or not interactive enough. Suggestions: make them more hands-on or replace Q&A sessions with coding walkthroughs -> these can be revamped a bit indeed
  • Tools like Protégé felt outdated; students want more relevant technologies and coding practice with SPARQL, databases, or modern web data tools -> we will investigate this. However, Protégé is still a state of the art tool in this domain
  • Requests for sample or mock exams, more practice questions, and exam-style exercises to better prepare. -> we will look at this as well
  • Guest lecture had sound issues;
  • Practicals were sometimes seen as less useful or redundant. -> we will likely redesign the assignments/practical setup for next year

Dear all,

We have now calculated and posted the final grade based on the other rounded partial grades: partial exam grade, the assignment grade and project grade. see the syllabus for the grading rules. If you have passed the course, congratulations!

If one of these grades is lower than 6.0, you will receive a "NVD" or "NS", indicating that you did not pass a required part of the course or did not attend the exam respectively. In that case, your "final grade" is displayed as empty ("") in Canvas.

  • If you did not pass the exam, there will be a resit on 8 January, I will provide more information about this and how you can look at your previous exam result.
  • If you did not pass the project, but you submitted something we graded between 45-55 points you get an extra opportunity to improve your project. This will count as a resit, the deadline is  8 January. If you are interested in this opportunity, please send us a message (kandd-staff@googlegroups.com) with the following information: 1) Your name and group number 2) Which members of your group want to do this project improvement resit 3) What your plan is to improve the final project (2-3 sentences)
  • The assignments can not be retaken, you will have to retake the course next year. Note that you can take the other partial grades with you to next year. 

If any errors were made in the grade calculation, let me know by email immediately.  I will submit the grades to VUNet on Monday.

Thanks for joining the course this year, we really hope you enjoyed it and learned something!

Victor

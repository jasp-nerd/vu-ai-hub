Rules for discussions (please read before posting/commenting)

We use this Discussion board for questions/discussion about the practical assignments and quizzes. We (TAs and course staff) will do our best to answer your questions, but we also encourage you to answer other student's questions. You can also ask such questions in person to the TAs. Below are a few rules of engagement:

  • Be constructive and use professional language when posting questions and answers. This message board is official VU communication channel and any abuse, discrimination, intimidating and undesired behaviour is not tolerated. (see also VU student charter)
  • When asking a question:
    • first quickly check if your question has already been asked by a fellow student, or whether it is listed in the Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ).
    • use a descriptive title such that your problem is clear and that fellow students can easily find your thread.
    • be as complete as possible in your question; include code snippets, screenshots, full error messages, etc.
    • wait at least 24 hours for a solution before sending your question to a TA (unless it impacts an immediate submission)
  • Community interaction is awarded: it is greatly appreciated if you post an answer to a question that you know a possible solution for. Students who contribute in a positively way may be awarded up to 5 (out of 100) extra credit on their final grade.

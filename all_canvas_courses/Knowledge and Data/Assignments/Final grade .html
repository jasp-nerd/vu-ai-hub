Final grade

Here you find your final grade, which is based on the exam grade, the assignment grade and project grade. Each of these three are rounded to the nearest .5 (except for the 5-6 range, which are rounded to whole grades). To pass the course, all three grades should be 6.0 or higher. In that case, your final grade is the average of the three, rounded to .5. To those that passed the course: congratulations!

If one of these grades is lower, you will receive a "NVD" or "NS", indicating that you did not pass a required part of the course or did not attend the exam respectively.

In that case, your "final grade" is displayed as empty "" in Canvas.

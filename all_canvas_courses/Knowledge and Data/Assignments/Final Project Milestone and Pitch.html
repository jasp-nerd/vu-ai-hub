Final Project Milestone and Pitch

Due: 10/10/2025, 11:59:00 PM

For this milestone, you need to deliver a draft version of the final project report with initial results for the first design of the data analysis pipeline, the external ontology, the two datasets (one RDF, one non-RDF) that you will use as well as a design for your own ontology. The points for this milestone assignment will be copied to the final project grade rubric.

Use the final project template: https://tinyurl.com/kandd24projecttemplate 

In the appropriate separate sections (1-3), provide a description of:

  1. the goal, motivation and stakeholders of the analysis: for whom is the analysis intended? How does the analysis satisfy a need of the stakeholders?
  2. A set of competency questions that describe what the knowledge graph and visualisation pipeline should address
  3. Identification of at least one external ontology that you can reuse, and motivate your choice. In some cases, it might be difficult to find an ontology that is closely related to the analysis domain. Then you can use more general ontologies and vocabularies, such as FOAF, DBO, VOCAB.ORG, SCHEMA.ORG, DBPedia ontology, etc.
  4. Identification of at least one external RDF datasource accessible through a SPARQL endpoint to populate your ontology
  5. Identification of at least one external non-RDF datasource that you will be able to convert to RDF data later on 
  6. A preliminary description of the methodology, domain and scope as well as the conceptualisation of the ontology, including at least 10 classes, at least 5 properties and 2 restrictions (200-300 words)
  7. If you have already initial versions of the other sections and/or the jupyter notebook, these can be submitted as well.

 

Next to this, you will also have to pitch the project to your peers and TA team in the Week 7 practical session. The milestone + pitch grade will count towards the final project.

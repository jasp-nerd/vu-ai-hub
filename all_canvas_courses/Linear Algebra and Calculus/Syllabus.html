Assessment

There will be an optional Calculus only exam on the 17th of November that will count towards 30% of the total grade. It will be done on the 17th of November at 11am.

The day of the final exam you will have the chance to choose between doing a full course exam (overriding your Calculus midterm grade if you took it and hence counting for the total of your grade) or a Linear Algebra only exam which will count towards a 70% of your grade along with the Calculus midterm. You will receive the full exam, but will be able to choose to do only one part of it. In both cases you will have the same amount of time. To pass the course you will need a total of 5.5, that is, you don't need a 5.5 in each part.

The exam will be on the 16th of December at 8:30.

The resit will consist of mandatory Calculus and Linear Algebra parts, making up a total of a 30% and a 70% of the resit respectively.

Material and Literature

For this course we have a feature for those who'd like to improve their use of the theory lectures. Incomplete notes! But don't panic! For each week you will have a PDF with the notes for each lesson, however, the notes have gaps that you will be able to fill with the information I provide in class by bringing them in your tablet or by printing them. In this way you can still take your own notes, make your own drawings, pay attention to the explanations and improve your learning experience while having a minimum guideline of the material.

Does that mean you are forced to come to class and use those notes otherwise you're out of material? Absolutely not! The notes are there to encourage students to come to the lectures and pay attention to detail, however, if you cannot make it, or you simply want to study on your own or use your own material even if you come to the sessions, you still have the books where the notes have been condensed from, so you won't miss a piece of information. Even so, the full notes will be uploaded afterwards.

The books on which the lectures and tutorials are based upon are:

Calculus A Complete Course (Ninth Edition) by Robert A. Adams and Cristopher Essex.

Linear Algebra and Its Applications (Sixth Edition) by David C. Lay, Steven R. Lay and Judi J. McDonald.

Final Demo Session

The grade of the demo session is based on the following criteria:

Content
The demo shows all aspects of the project. Functionalities and limitations are discussed.

Motivation
The motivation and purpose of the project is clearly explained.
Use of demo
The demo is used to supplement the talk. Features are demonstrated or you are guided to try them yourself.
Oral Communication
Clearly spoken. Students adjust to signals from audience
Discussion
The students are able to answer all questions.

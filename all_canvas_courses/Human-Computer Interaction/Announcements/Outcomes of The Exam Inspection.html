Dear All,

Yesterday, during the exam inspection, many of you requested to know if your grade qualified for passing the exam.

Most of the confusion stemmed from students between 23 and 26 points because 23.1 is 55% of 42 points, while 26.58 were the required points to pass the exam.

The reason and calculation from which 26.58 is derived is explained in thorough detail in the announcement from the 6th of May, "Exam results - please read carefully"; to summarise the points from that announcement:

  • 11.16 exam points are regarded as chance correction (based on TestVision calculations).
  • The passing amount of points (26.58) is derived from: 
    • total points minus chance correction: 42 - 11.16 = 30.84 
    • --> 50% of which is: 30.84 * 0.5 = 15.42 
    • --> with chance points: 15.42 + 11.16 = 26.58
  • The grade out of 10 for the exam is calculated as: grade = 1 + 9*((total_points_scored - 11.16) / (42.0 - 11.16))

This sets 26.58 points to the expected passing grade of 5.5 (while getting 42 points grants you full points, aka 10/10).

TestVision automatically calculates all of this and is thus free of human error.

Policy regarding late arrivals and early departures during practicals

Posted: 5/7/2025, 5:43:51 PM

Dear students, 

Your participation in the practicals is mandatory, and this includes the entire 1.5 hour allocated to the practical session. TAs are instructed to mark the attendance of all team members at the beginning of the practical session (at 15:30). 

Please note:

  • If you arrive more than 10 minutes late, your attendance will no longer be changed, and you will be marked as absent for that practical.
  • You cannot leave the practical before the end time (17:15) even if your group is done with all the assignment questions (which is unlikely). 
  • If you depart early, your attendance will be marked as an absence for that day.
  • You can take a break of 15 minutes somewhere in between the practical session. This can be when you need it, or you think the discussion is at a good place for a pause. However, skipping a break does not give you grounds for departing the practical early.

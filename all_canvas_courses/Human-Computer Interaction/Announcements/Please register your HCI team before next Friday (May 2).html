Posted: 4/26/2025, 12:37:09 AM

Dear students,

Now that the exam is behind us, it's time to prepare for the second part of the course: practical sessions and the group project.

 

The 5 practical sessions in this course will be conducted in teams of 4 students. From the very first practical session (May 7), you will be working on your group project (and Assignment 3) together with your group members. Therefore, you must have already formed a group that is assigned to a room and a TA by then. 

Please form a team of 4 people and enroll it on Canvas, People > HCI Teams.

All group members must assign themselves to a specified team number by joining the team. You cannot add/enroll another student to your team. If you enrol in one team but decide to change it, Canvas will allow you to unenroll and join a different team. If you are a team of two people, try to find another team of two people and register yourself there. If you are a single person with no team yet, please try to find a team with 3 members and join them rather than assigning yourself as a solo member of a new group. 

 

NOTE: Resit students must form a team together and assign themselves to the Teams at the bottom of the list named "HCI Team # (Resit)". Resit students cannot mix with first-year students for the group project. All resit students must participate in the practicals and submit the assignments and the final project report again. As indicated in the syllabus, component grades from last year cannot be transferred. 

 

Please register your team by next Friday (May 2nd) so that we have enough time to assign all teams to their corresponding TAs. Then you will know which room you need to go to for the first practical session on May 7th.

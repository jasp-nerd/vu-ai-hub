Dear students,

 

One of my students in the 3rd year of the AI program is conducting a study on Brain-Computer Interface (BCI) games as part of her thesis project. She is currently looking for participants to collect data. If you are interested in controlling a fantasy game with your brain signals, please consider participating in her experiment. You can also see what collecting brain signals looks like in a human-computer interaction setup. 

The experiment will take about 1.5 hours and will be conducted in our research lab in the NU building.

Conditions: You can only participate in this experiment if you are at least 18 years old. You cannot participate in the study if you have any neurological or neurodevelopmental disorders, such as epilepsy, MS, autism, or ADHD.

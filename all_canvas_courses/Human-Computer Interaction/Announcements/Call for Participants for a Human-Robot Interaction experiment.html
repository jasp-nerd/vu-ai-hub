Dear HCI students,

 

Two of my master's students are conducting a study on neuro-adaptive robots. If you are interested in seeing how brain signals are collected during a human-robot collaboration task, please fill out the following form to sign up for the study. You can see our robot lab and EEG equipment, and also receive a 10-euro bol gift card upon completing the experiment. 

https://bit.ly/EEG-Experiment

 

Note: Unfortunately, you cannot participate in the study if you have any neurological/neurodevelopmental disorders, such as epilepsy, MS, autism, or ADHD. This is to ensure that the brain responses we collect are not affected by other conditions.

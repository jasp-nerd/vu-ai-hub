Dear Students,

Your final course grades have now been calculated and published on Canvas. Apologies for the delay; this was due to the time needed to finalize a grading script that accurately accounted for all course requirements and conditions.

To help you understand how your final course grade was determined, we’ve added 10 new columns in the Grade Center. These provide a transparent breakdown of the grading logic:

  1. Has Passed Attendance (0 or 1)

  2. Has Passed Assignment (0 or 1)

  3. Has Passed Exam or Resit (0 or 1)

  4. Has Passed Final Report (0 or 1)

  5. Best Exam Grade (out of 10)

  6. Final Report Grade (out of 10)

  7. Late Submission Penalty (negative value)

  8. Resit Submission Penalty (negative value)

  9. Final Course Grade Before Rounding (out of 10)

  10. Final Course Grade After Rounding (rounded to the nearest half)

How Your Final Grade Was Calculated:

  1. Initial Conditions:
    You needed to receive a 1 (pass) in both “Has Passed Attendance” and “Has Passed Assignment” to be eligible for a final course grade.

    • If you received a 0 in either, your final grade was automatically set to 1, meaning you did not meet the course requirements and thus failed the course.

  2. Grade computation:
    If you passed both attendance and assignments:

    • We then considered your Best Exam Grade and your Report Grade.

    • If you failed either the exam/resit or the final report (i.e., a 0 in the relevant column), your final grade equals the grade of the failed component.

  3. If You Passed All Components:
    We used the following formula to compute your final course grade before rounding:

    Final Course Grade Before Rounding = (Best Exam Grade + Final Report Grade) / 2 + Late Submissions Penalty + Resit Submissions Penalty

     

  4. Rounding Rule:
    Final grades were then rounded according to VU guidelines:

    • Grades between 5.0 (inclusive) and 5.5 (exclusive) were rounded down to 5

    • Grades between 5.5 (inclusive) and 6.0 (inclusive) were rounded up to 6

    • All other grades were rounded to the nearest 0.5
      (e.g., 7.15 → 7, 8.76 → 9, 6.25 → 6.5)

About Penalties:

Penalty scores were applied based on late or incomplete assignment submissions, as outlined in the syllabus. A 5% penalty (- 0.5 point) was applied to your final course grade for every late or incomplete submission you had to one of the four assignments.

Your Task:


Please review your grade breakdown carefully to ensure that your final course grade has been calculated correctly. If you notice any discrepancies, let us know as soon as possible.

We will submit the final grades for registration on Friday, July 18, which is the official deadline to ensure your BSA letters can be prepared on time. Unfortunately, we will not be able to process any grade-related concerns received after July 18.

Dear HCI teams,

 

Please do not forget to submit your report for Assignment 3 before midnight tomorrow. 

 

Also, please note that we take the usage of gen AI in assignment reports very seriously! As indicated in the syllabus, you can use GPT and other AI chatbots as an editing tool that helps you with refining and structuring your text, but you cannot pass your assignment questions to these models, and copy the answer in its entirety. This is considered academic fraud.

 

These models are known for hallucination and the generation of fake information/references/etc. Examples of such fake citations were identified in some of the reports submitted for assignment 2. We offered a resit opportunity to those who failed the assignment because of this.

For assignment 3, we will not be lenient anymore and will not offer resit opportunities to fix AI-generated mistakes. If we spot traces of fake citations and AI-generated text, we will report the team (all members!) to the examination committee for suspected academic fraud.

Posted: 4/22/2025, 10:19:03 AM

Dear students,
All submissions for Assignment 1 have now been marked as either Complete or Incomplete. As outlined in the syllabus, we only checked whether you had attempted to answer all questions and whether your submission followed the required template.
However, we noticed that MANY of you used generative AI to prepare your submissions. This was clear as the examples provided for Question 1 were nearly identical across numerous submissions. In fact, Jacopo and I were able to reproduce the same examples simply by entering the question into ChatGPT. Similar patterns were found in Questions 2 and 3, where we saw ChatGPT-style phrasing, values, and tables.
Despite this, we kept our promise by grading your assignments as Complete, because—as stated in the syllabus:
"...assignments in this course serve as formative assessments to ensure that you follow the lecture content... The goal is for you to attempt the questions..."
Now, we understand that some of you may have used ChatGPT as a supplementary tool to help you work through the questions—this is acceptable, provided that you learned from this process. Others, however, may have fully outsourced the assignment to ChatGPT without trying the questions at all. In our view, these are two very different approaches. The first supports learning; the second undermines it and fails to meet the goals of the assignment.
That said, we consider you all adults capable of making your own choices about your education. If you choose to compromise your learning by relying entirely on AI tools without engaging with the content, that decision and its consequences rest with you.

Please keep in mind: you won’t have access to ChatGPT during the exam.

Dear students

 

Your exam grades are now posted in the Grades Center. As usual, all grades equal to or greater than 5.5 (>= 5.5) are considered a pass. 

  • Congratulations to those of you who passed the exam; you can now focus your energy on the group project and completing Assignments 3 & 4.
  • The students who did not pass the exam but passed the eligibility criteria so far (completed Assignments 1 and 2) can continue the course by participating in the practical sessions and working on the group project together with their HCI teams, however, they will need to take the resit exam on June 3rd and pass that exam. Otherwise, they will fail this course regardless of their project report grade.
  • The students who failed the exam and also did not meet the eligibility criteria to continue the course are not required to take the resit exam.  



Out of 225 students who took the exam, 165 students (73%) passed the exam. This is comparable to last year's results, where 76% of students passed the written exam on the first attempt. However, it should be noted that last year, students took the test at the end of the course when they also had the deadline for submission of the group project report and other course exams. We hoped that by separating the workload of the course into two parts and organizing the exam after the theoretical seminars, we would see better passing rates on the first attempt, creating more space for you to focus on the group project in the second part of the course. While this has not led to better passing rates on the exam, we still believe this structure will help most teams to focus on the group project and deliver better reports at the end of the course.


We will organize an inspection at TENT before the Resit exam for those of you who would like to review your exam results. The day and hour will be announced as soon as we book the room with the schedulers.

Please note the following regarding the exam questions and computation of the grade:

1) The grades are automatically computed by TestVision. This also includes chance-correction. The chance score is the number of points a student can get on this exam by just guessing or choosing the same answer (e.g., option "a") in all questions. The computed chance threshold for this test was 11.16 points out of 42 total points. After correcting for this chance score, you should have obtained 50% of the remaining points for a passing grade (i.e., total of 26.58 points or more).

2) After applying chance correction and a cut-off threshold at 50%, TestVision converts the grades to a tenth scale and rounds them down to the nearest 10th of a point (so a 7.66 becomes a 7.6). During the inspection, you have access to the total points earned on the exam and for each question. You can also compute your grade on the tenth scale yourself using the following formula:

Grade out of 10 = 1 + 9*((total_points_scored - 11.16) / (42.0 - 11.16))

Results of the resit exam are now posted

Posted: 6/11/2025, 5:47:45 PM

Dear students,

The grades for the resit exam, which took place on June 3rd, are now posted in the Grades Center. As usual, all grades equal to or greater than 5.5 (>= 5.5) are considered a pass.

Out of 59 students who took the resit exam, 53 students (90%) passed it. Congratulations to these students! The others who did not pass will need to take the course again next year.


Please note the following:

1) The grades are automatically computed and scaled from 1-10 by TestVision (no human intervention). This also includes chance correction. The total possible score for the resit exam was 40 points, with a chance score of 10.62 points (this is the score a student can get on this exam by just guessing or choosing the same answer, e.g., option "a" in all questions). After correcting for this chance score, you should have obtained at least 50% of the remaining points for a passing grade (i.e., 25.31 or more points out of the 40 points available in this exam).

2) If you took both the exam and its resit and obtained a passing grade on both exams, the highest grade you achieved will be considered for your Final Course Grade computation.

 

3) We are still in the process of grading the project reports. If you passed the exam (or its resit) as well as the group project, your Final Course Grade will be computed as follows: (more information will be shared once we post the grades for project reports)
Final Course Grade = 0.5 x Group Project Report + 0.5 x Exam Grade


3) If you would like to review your exam, please send an email to: m.alimardani@vu.nl
I will make an appointment and arrange for you to come to my office and review your exam.

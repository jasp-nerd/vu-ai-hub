Dear students, 

 

If you would like to review your exam, you can do so next week on May 21st (Wednesday), 12:15 - 13:15.

The reserved rooms for exam inspection are the following computer rooms:

  • (T) HG-02B02-H (23)
  • (T) HG-02B02-I (28)
  • (T) HG-02B02-J (23)

We highly recommend that you use this opportunity if you failed the exam and are planning to take the resit.
Note that the resit is scheduled on June 3, 18:45 - 21:00. See rooster.vu.nl for the details.

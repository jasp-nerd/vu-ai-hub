Posted: 6/25/2025, 5:51:47 PM

Dear Teams, 

 

 You can find your report grade (out of 100), the points you received on each criterion, and the motivations supporting the points on Canvas. The lecturer team and the head TA checked evaluations to make sure they align with the rubrics. 

Some teams had clearly invested a lot of effort into their project and prepared a high-quality report. I was happy to see such reports from the 1st year students. I hope you all enjoyed this research project and learned a lot from it. 

As always, grades equal to or larger than 55 are considered a pass, and grades lower than 55 are a fail. 

Luckily, this year, we did not have any teams that failed the project report component. So, congratulations to all of you for conducting a sound HCI experiment and preparing a valid report.

Note:

  • If you have questions regarding the grades and motivations you received, please get in touch with your TA and head TA first. If your concern is valid and a re-evaluation is deemed necessary, the head TA will discuss it with the lecturers.
  • In the coming days, we will finalize and register the course grades via VUnet. All grades will be determined in accordance with the assessment rules outlined in the course syllabus.

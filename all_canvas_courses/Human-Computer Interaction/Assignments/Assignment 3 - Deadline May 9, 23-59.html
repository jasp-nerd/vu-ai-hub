Questions related to the group project

In this course, you are expected to conduct a group project where you design and evaluate a human-agent interaction following the themes proposed on this page. Before you design an interaction and start prototyping, you should first define your interaction goals (what problem are you trying to solve?), target user group (who are you developing the interaction for?), and research questions (what variables and aspects of the interaction are you manipulating and evaluating in your user study?). In classic HCI literature, this is called the use case definition. Of course, your use case can be refined and modified in the following weeks when you start prototyping and identify usability issues (you can report such modifications in the use case scenario in the following assignment reports, so no need to resubmit this assignment in case of a change in your initial plans).

In this assignment, we will help you define your HAI/HRI project by answering the following questions:

Question 1. Use case description (100-250 words)
Give a high-level overview of what your prospective HRI/HAI is about. What theme did you choose, and what problem within this theme do you plan to address? How do you plan to design the interaction so that the agent/robot would alleviate the problem?

Question 2. Target user group(s) (100-250 words)
Describe the target users you are developing the interaction for (young children? university students? older adults? etc.). Try to make this description as specific as possible by providing demographics (e.g., age, gender, race, education level, etc.), psychographic information (e.g., personality, interests, attitude toward X, etc.), cognitive and physical attributes (especially in health-related projects), motivations and expectations of your target audience. 

Question 3. Interaction scenario (200-500 words)
Provide an example of the interaction flow between an individual from your target user group and the conversational agent. What is the task of the user? What is the role of the agent in this interaction? What form of communication will the agent engage in (verbal, non-verbal, or both)? How long is the interaction expected to last? etc.

Question 4. Literature review (200-500)
Provide 3 or 4 relevant studies that you found in the literature with a similar theme and RQs as your project. List the studies you found in APA referencing format. and for each give a short paragraph summarizing the outcome of the study. 

Question 5. Envisioned challenges (200-300 words)
You may want to address all the needs of your target users in one interaction flow, however, please be advised that every communication skill and functionality of your social agent requires prototyping and validation. Therefore, we highly recommend that you only focus on one interaction aspect/element in your project and try to solely evaluate the impact of that element on your target users. Here, you must identify and discuss the challenges and difficulties associated with the design and development of HAI/HRI for the specific user group targeted in your project. Describe the identified challenges in terms of the user needs and tasks, and consequently, the expected functionalities from your social agent.

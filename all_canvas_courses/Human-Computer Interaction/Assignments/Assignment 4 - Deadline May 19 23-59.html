Question 3: Pilot study

Conduct a small (pilot) user experiment to evaluate your lo-fi prototype. The goal of this pilot is to check the functionality of your prototype. The insights you gather should allow you to identify the errors and shortcomings of your prototype so that you can design an improved Hi-Fi prototype next week.

Before you start your pilot experiment, it is a good practice to define your "study protocol".  Such a protocol is a recipe-like description with step-by-step instructions that define each part of your pilot study. So think of a numbered list that makes it clear to all experimenters who does what and in what order. Be concrete! So if step one is: "to welcome the participant", then, in your protocol, make clear who is reading the welcome text, and what text is used (e.g., you want exactly the same text for all your participants to make sure this does not cause any variation in the outcomes). 

Once you develop the protocol, conduct the user study with at least 3 peers from your TA's session (these should be unbiased people who haven't seen your prototype before, therefore, you cannot conduct the study with the members of your own team).

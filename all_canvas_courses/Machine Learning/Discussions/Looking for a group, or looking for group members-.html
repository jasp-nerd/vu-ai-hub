Looking for a group, or looking for group members?

Everybody is free to join any group that still has space.

However, sometimes it's nice to be in a group with people of a similar level of ambition, or who like the thing you plan to work on.

This thread is for the following kinds of situations:

  • You cannot come to campus, or you'd like to meet on campus if possible, and you'd like a group with a similar mindset.
  • You expect you won't be able to put too much time into the course, and you're looking for a group of people who won't mind you putting in only minimal effort
  • You'd like time to focus on the exam, so you want to lighten the workload on the project side.
  • You have a (general) idea about what you'd like to do, and you're looking for group members who are on board with that.
  • Your group wants to do something ambitious, and you're looking for people with a similar attitude.
  • You don't like the idea of joining a random group and you want to join one that's happy to have a random person join them.

So if you don't feel like joining a random group, or if you're worried that random joiners might not like your plan, post a message here.

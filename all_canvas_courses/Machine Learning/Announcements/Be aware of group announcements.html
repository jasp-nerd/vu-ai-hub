Hi all,

A quick note on communication. When something happens that only affects one homework group, like a session cancelled at the last minute or a room change, we will let you know about this via a group announcement. This is an announcement you only see if you are in that specific homework group.

If you have Canvas set up so that announcements are sent to your email, you'll get these along with the regular announcements. However, some of you only see the announcements on Canvas, where the group announcements are a bit hidden. You can access the Group homepage under People > Groups and then click on the three dots next to your group to go to the group homepage.

For project groups the same holds. If a TA needs to communicate with your group they will usually post an announcement to the project group homepage.

regards,
  Peter

PS. If you want the announcements sent to your email or phone, you can set this up under Account > Notifications.

Dear all,

The grades for Quiz 1 should now be available for everyone.

Some of you may wonder how the grade was established, or why you didn't get more points. Sadly, we don't have the resources to leave feedback for each individual open question (bear in mind that one quiz point is just 1/160th of your final grade for the course).

Instead, we will provide collective feedback. During the next homework session, the TA will take ~10 minutes (at the start) to discuss some common mistakes that were made and some things we saw that deserved extra points. If that doesn't clarify your grade for you, you can, if there's time, show your answer to the homework TA to get some feedback from them. Note however, that your homework TA is not the person who graded the quiz. That information is not made public. 

Finally, if you're really not satisfied with your score, you can contact me to request a regrade. A different TA will go over your quiz, and regrade it carefully at the end of the course. Note however, that in this case, the second grade always counts, even if it's lower. We have limited capacity, so please don't request this lightly.

Those are your options: ask a TA for feedback, or ask me for a regrade (or both). See the page On grading and feedback for a more extensive explanation of our reasoning and method.

with kind regards,
  Peter

PS. For the final report, you will receive feedback. It's just not feasible for us to do it for the quizzes.

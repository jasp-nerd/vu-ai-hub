On retake quizzes

Posted: 3/9/2026, 10:41:27 AM

Dear all,

Most of you have now finished the quizzes and received all grades. This leaves the question for some, of whether you should do a retake quiz.

First of all, bear in mind that if you do a retake (one or more), the penalty is a cap on your examination grade (quizzes + exam) of a 7. That means that if you have a chance of scoring higher than a 7, you may be wondering whether it's smart to do a retake.

My advice is to wait until after the exam to make the decision. You can calculate your examination score on the day of the exam (Monday), after which you will still have a full week to complete any retakes you may need, or don't if you already score above a 7.

However, for this to work it's important that you copy your exam answers onto the exam booklet and take them home with you. This is allowed, and we publish the correct answers on the day of the exam so you can check right away how you did. The official grade often takes more than a week to arrive, so if you wait for that one, the quiz retake deadline will have passed.

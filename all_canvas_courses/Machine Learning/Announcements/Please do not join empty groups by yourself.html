Please do not join empty groups by yourself

Posted: 2/3/2026, 10:00:09 AM

Dear all,

It's good to see so many people making groups. Unfortunately, we currently have some people in a group by themselves, and very few empty groups left. This makes it difficult for people to register the groups they want to form.

If you are currently in a group by yourself, please join another group with other students in it already. Have a look at the discussion board to see if there are groups actively looking for members. Otherwise, just pick any group and introduce yourself.

We reserve the right to remove people from solo groups (without warning) if these groups are blocking larger groups from forming.

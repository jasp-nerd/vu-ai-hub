Quizzes: corrections and clarifications

It's likely that we'll discover small mistakes in the quizzes occasionally after they've been made available, or things that should be clarified. To avoid a long string of announcements, we will add these corrections here.

We will fix all mistakes, but if you start the quiz before the correction, you will still see the old version. The note will tell you how to answer the question. Note that the questions are randomized, so you may not have received the question with the mistake.

If there's something wrong with one of the questions and you don't see it here, please send your TA a message.

General

When you submit a quiz, Canvas shows you a number of points, which is usually low. This is because the open questions have not yet been marked. The points will go up when we finish marking.

Please do not use the browser Safari to do the quizzes. If you encounter any technical trouble, try to close the quiz without submitting and open it again in another browser.

Read the page on grading and feedback for an in-depth explanation of our philosophy on grading, and how you can get feedback.

In essay questions, you may feel like you're required to take a position you don't agree with in order to get a good grade. This is not the case. You may take whatever position you think is correct, and you will be judged on the soundness and clarity of your argument. Often, it's best not to take a very strong position at all, and to enumerate different perspectives on a question.

If, for any reason you feel like you are not able to answer a question, because doing so would clash with your convictions, or require you to elaborate on your views in a way you don't feel comfortable doing, you can give a clear and honest reasoning of why you've chosen not to answer, and we will grade that instead.

The quizzing system in Canvas is a little brittle. We recommend saving your answers somewhere outside of canvas, especially the mini-essays, so that you can easily re-use them if you are given another attempt at the quiz to fix some mistake. Canvas does not allow us to re-open a submitted quiz and save the answers.

Quiz 2

The aim of the essay question is for you to show how well you can anticipate what questions and problems might crop up. We can't tell which questions and which potential problems to discuss, since coming up with those is the aim of the question. Try to think of as many perspectives and details as possible, over the whole data science project from start to finish. Think of social problems, technical problems, data quality issues, etc. Then, since there is a word limit, make a selection that shows a wide variety of different questions and problems you might encounter, while also showing that you've thought through the details.
